## The question

How can we investigate failures in multi-agent systems in a way that people can inspect and challenge?

## The approach

For a CMU Responsible AI and AI Governance project, I developed a four-agent LangGraph system with SHA-256 audit trails and human review. The project compared LLM-based and rule-based classifiers and evaluated findings through the NIST AI Risk Management Framework and EU AI Act principles.

My research resume reports a 37 percentage point F1 improvement in the comparison. I want this page to eventually explain the evaluation setup, baseline, limitations, and what that result means.
