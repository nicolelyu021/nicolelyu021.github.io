## The idea

Pinlog starts with a map: plan a trip as pins, add photos and notes to those places, and generate a short vlog from the journey. The repository describes it as a team project for HackCMU 2026.

The project brings together a web interface, location-aware photo organization, AI-assisted planning and writing, and video generation.

## My contribution

My main contribution was the idea and pitch. It came from a personal frustration: travel photos often sit in my camera roll instead of becoming something I can share. I proposed a tool that helps people turn those moments into a creative travel vlog, and I convinced my teammates to build around that direction.
