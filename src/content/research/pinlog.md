## The idea

Pinlog starts with a map: plan a trip as pins, add photos and notes to those places, and generate a short vlog from the journey. The repository describes it as a team project for HackCMU 2026.

The project brings together a web interface, location-aware photo organization, AI-assisted planning and writing, and video generation. Nicole's specific contribution and the final demo outcome should be added after review.
