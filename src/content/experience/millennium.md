During my AI/ML infrastructure internship, I built Runway, a self-service portal for people across the organization, especially business users with working code but little deployment experience. A process that typically took about a week could be completed through the portal in roughly 15 minutes, from local code to a live Kubernetes application.

I designed pieces of the delivery system around Docker, Helm, GitHub Actions, Artifactory, Argo CD, and Kubernetes. The experience sharpened how I think about reliability, security, and the handoff between infrastructure experts and the people using their tools.
